Return & Exchange Policy

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If you are not satisfied with an eligible product, you may request a return according to the conditions below.

1. Return Period

Customers may request a return within 7 days of receiving the order unless a product listing specifies a different return period.

2. Eligible Returns

  • Product must be unused and in resalable condition.
  • Original packaging, accessories and labels should be included.
  • Product should not show damage caused by misuse.
  • Proof of purchase/order information may be required.

3. Non-Returnable Products

  • Personalized or customized products.
  • Products marked as non-returnable on the product page.
  • Items damaged through customer misuse.
  • Products missing essential accessories or packaging.

4. Damaged or Incorrect Product

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, contact customer support as soon as possible. Photos or videos may be requested to verify the issue.

5. Exchange

Exchanges may be offered subject to product availability. If the replacement is unavailable, an eligible refund may be issued.

Refund Policy

Refund Approval

Refunds are processed after the returned product has been received and inspected. Approval depends on the return conditions being satisfied.

Refund Method

Approved refunds are generally issued to the original payment method. The time taken for the amount to appear can depend on the customer's bank or payment provider.

Shipping Charges

Original shipping charges may not be refundable unless the return is caused by an incorrect, defective or damaged product.

Shipping Policy

Order Processing

Orders are normally processed after payment confirmation. Processing and delivery times can vary depending on product availability and delivery location.

Delivery Delays

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed. Delays can occur due to weather, transportation issues, holidays, courier problems or other events outside our control.

Incorrect Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address. Additional shipping costs may apply if an order must be reshipped because of an incorrect address.
