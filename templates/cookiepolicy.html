Cookie Policy

Learn how we use cookies and similar technologies on our website.

Last Updated: September 4, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit and provide certain functionality.

We may also use similar technologies such as pixels, local storage and other tracking technologies.

2. How We Use Cookies

YourStore may use cookies for purposes including:

  • Keeping products in your shopping cart.
  • Keeping you signed in to your account.
  • Remembering website preferences.
  • Improving website performance.
  • Understanding how visitors use our website.
  • Detecting security problems and fraudulent activity.
  • Supporting relevant advertising where applicable.

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

Cookie Type Purpose Typical Use
Essential Required for core website functionality. Shopping cart, login, security and checkout.
Preferences Remember choices made by visitors. Language, region and website preferences.
Analytics Help us understand website traffic and usage. Page visits, traffic sources and performance.
Marketing May be used to provide or measure relevant advertising. Advertising and campaign measurement.

4. Essential Cookies

Essential cookies may be necessary for the website to function correctly. For example, they may help maintain your shopping cart, process checkout or protect the website against security threats.

Depending on applicable law, these cookies may not require the same type of consent as optional cookies.

5. Analytics Cookies

Analytics technologies may help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

This information may be used to improve website navigation, performance and customer experience.

6. Marketing Cookies

Where used and permitted by applicable law, marketing cookies may help us or our advertising partners deliver, personalize or measure advertisements.

Marketing cookies should only be activated in accordance with the consent requirements applicable to your visitors.

7. Third-Party Cookies

Some services integrated into our website may place their own cookies. These may include payment services, analytics providers, advertising services, social-media features or other third-party technologies.

Third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. How You Can Control Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also use the cookie preferences provided on our website, where available.

Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality, including shopping cart and checkout features.

9. Cookie Consent

Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential cookies, we will request consent before activating those cookies.

You may withdraw or change your cookie choices where the applicable cookie-consent mechanism provides that option.

10. Privacy Policy

Cookies may involve the processing of information that can be associated with a visitor or device.

For more information about how we handle personal information, please read our Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, technologies, services or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
