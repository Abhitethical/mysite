Shipping Policy

Important information about our delivery service.

📦 Order Processing

Orders are normally processed within 1–2 business days. Orders placed during weekends or holidays will be processed on the next business day.

🚚 Delivery Times

Delivery times depend on the shipping method selected during checkout. Estimated delivery times do not include order processing time.

🌍 International Shipping

International shipping may be available for selected countries. Additional customs duties, taxes or fees may apply depending on the destination.

🏠 Incorrect Address

Please make sure your shipping address is correct before completing your order. We may not be able to change the address after the order has shipped.

📭 Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package is lost or arrives damaged, please contact our customer support team as soon as possible so we can investigate the issue.

🎁 Delivery Delays

Weather, holidays, carrier delays and other unexpected circumstances may occasionally affect delivery times.
